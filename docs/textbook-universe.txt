STANDARD COSMOLOGICAL MODEL – ΛCDM (TEXTBOOK BASELINE, COMPACT)

1. ONTOLOGY
- Universe is well-described by a homogeneous, isotropic 3D space on large scales.
- Spacetime is a 4D manifold governed by General Relativity.
- Singularities appear in the math but are treated as limits of the theory.

2. GRAVITY
- Gravity = curvature of spacetime (Einstein).
- Described by Einstein Field Equations.
- No confirmed quantum theory of gravity; GR used at all observable scales.

3. FORCES
- Four known interactions: EM, Weak, Strong, Gravity.
- Electroweak unification accepted.
- Strong force remains separate; GUTs are theoretical and unconfirmed.

4. COSMOLOGY (EXPANSION)
- Expansion governed by Friedmann equations under GR.
- ΛCDM is the standard model of cosmology.
- Λ (cosmological constant) has equation of state w = -1.
- Composition today:
  - ~70% dark energy (Λ)
  - ~25% cold dark matter
  - ~5% baryonic matter
- Expansion is accelerating due to Λ.

5. SPACETIME + QUANTUM FIELDS
- Quantum fields propagate on a classical curved spacetime background.
- No requirement that spacetime be emergent.
- Entanglement plays no structural role in standard GR.

6. BLACK HOLES
- Black holes have a classical interior region.
- Event horizon is a causal boundary, not a physical surface.
- Information paradox unresolved; holography is a theoretical tool, not required.
- Entropy ∝ horizon area (Bekenstein–Hawking).

7. GLOBAL HOLOGRAPHY
- Holographic principle is a theoretical framework (AdS/CFT).
- Not assumed to apply to our universe.
- No requirement that the cosmos has a holographic boundary.

8. STRUCTURE + GEOMETRY
- Large-scale structure arises from initial density fluctuations + gravity.
- Cold dark matter drives structure formation.
- Geometry determined by matter-energy content via Einstein equations.

9. DESIGN PRINCIPLES
- GR is the correct theory of gravity at all tested scales.
- ΛCDM is the correct large-scale cosmological model.
- Dark matter and dark energy are treated as real components.
- No commitment to quantum gravity, GUT, or holography.

10. STATUS SUMMARY
- Gravity: classical GR.
- Forces: EM, Weak, Strong, Gravity.
- Expansion: ΛCDM with accelerating scale factor.
- Black holes: classical interior + horizon; holography optional.
- Spacetime: classical manifold; quantum fields live on it.
