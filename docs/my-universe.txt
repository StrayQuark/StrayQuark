STRAYQUARK COSMOLOGICAL MODEL – v3.2 (COMPACT)

1. ONTOLOGY
- Universe is finite with a global 3-manifold shape.
- Spacetime is classical geometry (Einstein curvature).
- No physical infinities; singularities are math artifacts.

2. GRAVITY
- Gravity is NOT a quantum force or quantum field.
- No gravitons, no quantum gravity, no renormalization.
- Quantum fields exist on a classical curved background.

3. FORCES
- Only three quantum gauge forces: EM, Weak, Strong.
- Electroweak unification accepted.
- No GUT; no unification of Strong with Electroweak.
- Gravity is separate: geometry, not a gauge interaction.

4. COSMOLOGY (EXPANSION)
- Use standard ΛCDM for expansion history.
- Λ with equation of state w = -1.
- Effective composition: ~70% Λ, ~25% DM, ~5% baryonic.
- Λ treated as phenomenological; deeper origin unspecified.

5. SPACETIME + ENTANGLEMENT
- Spacetime connectivity linked to quantum entanglement structure.
- Time = growth of entanglement complexity.
- Geometry behaves classically; entanglement picture is interpretive, not a quantum gravity theory.

6. BLACK HOLES
- Classical interior (GR).
- Event horizon is holographic (Susskind / ’t Hooft / Maldacena).
- Information encoded on the horizon; entropy ∝ area.
- ER=EPR allowed as interpretation; does not remove interior.

7. GLOBAL HOLOGRAPHY
- Black hole horizons are holographic.
- Universe as a whole not required to be holographic.
- Cosmological holography left open, not assumed.

8. STRUCTURE + GEOMETRY
- Large-scale structure reflects curvature + matter distribution.
- Einstein equations govern geometry.
- Entanglement view compatible but secondary to classical GR.

9. DESIGN PRINCIPLES
- No quantum gravity.
- No gravitons.
- No GUT.
- No physical infinities.
- No forced “one force” unification.
- No required global holography.

10. STATUS SUMMARY
- Gravity: classical curvature.
- Forces: EM, Weak, Strong only.
- Expansion: ΛCDM.
- Black holes: classical interior + holographic horizon.
- Spacetime: classical GR with optional entanglement interpretation.
